## Persona — Solo Developer

**Name:** Ethan
**Role:** Non-technical Founder
**Tag:** "I have an idea and I need to build it."

Ethan is a business major working on his own app. He is not a CS student and 
does not live in GitHub. He knows what he wants to build but struggles to 
stay on top of all the moving pieces while also learning to code.

### Pain Points

- GitHub feels overwhelming and built for engineers, not him
- Loses track of what still needs to get done between coding sessions
- Writing detailed issues takes time he does not have
- No easy way to delegate a task to AI and come back to a finished result

### User Stories

- As a solo founder, I want to quickly log what needs to get done, in order to stay organized without a complicated setup
- As a solo founder, I want to assign tasks to an AI agent, in order to make progress even when I am not coding
- As a solo founder, I want to review what the agent completed, in order to make sure it matches what I had in mind
- As a solo founder, I want all my tasks in one simple dashboard, in order to always know what is open and what is done
- As a solo founder, I want my workspace to be private, in order to keep my app idea and progress to myself
