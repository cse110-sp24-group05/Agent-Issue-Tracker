**Personas and User Stories**

**Persona 1: Developer \- Ethan**   
 A CS student developer who spends most of his time coding and wants to stay focused without interruptions.

**Pain Points**

* Forgets to log issues while coding  
* Issues don’t match the actual bug or problem  
* Switching between tools (GitHub, Slack) breaks focus  
* Writing issues feels repetitive and time-consuming

**User Stories**

* As a developer, I want to create an issue directly from an error in order to not lose context while coding  
* As a developer, I want AI to fill in issue details in order to save time and effort  
* As a developer, I want to see my next task immediately in order to stay in flow

**Persona 2: AI Agent \- AIT Agent**  
 An AI assistant built into the issue tracker that helps create, organize, and update issues automatically.

**Pain Points**

* Issues are written in messy, unstructured text  
* Missing important details (files, errors, expected output)  
* Hard to tell when a task is actually complete  
* No tracking of token or compute usage

**User Stories**

* As an AI agent, I want issues in a structured format in order to execute tasks correctly  
* As an AI agent, I want to update issue status automatically in order to reflect progress without human input  
* As an AI agent, I want to track token usage per issue in order to manage resources efficiently

**Persona 3: Manager \- Adam**

 A student team lead responsible for keeping the team on track and ensuring deadlines are met.

**Pain Points**

* Hard to tell who is actually working on what  
* Team members forget to update issue status  
* Cannot easily identify blocked or delayed tasks  
* Uses multiple tools to track progress

**User Stories**

* As a manager, I want to see all team tasks in one place  
* As a manager, I want to quickly identify blocked or overdue tasks  
* As a manager, I want AI to suggest task priorities

**Persona 4: TA / Customer \-  Elena**

 A course teaching assistant who evaluates teams based on their workflow, documentation, and process.

**Pain Points**

* Cannot clearly see who contributed what  
* Issues are incomplete or poorly documented  
* Difficult to track team progress over time  
* Teams focus more on features than process

**User Stories**

* As a TA, I want to view issue history to evaluate team process  
* As a TA, I want clearly structured and documented issues  
* As a TA, I want to quickly understand overall project progress
